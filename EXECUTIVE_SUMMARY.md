# Onboard Flow - Executive Summary

## 🎯 What We Built

An **AI-powered onboarding platform** that automatically transforms company PDF documents into interactive, guided onboarding experiences.

---

## ⚡ The Problem

Traditional onboarding is:
- **Time-consuming** - 10-20 hours to create materials manually
- **Expensive** - High HR overhead for setup and maintenance
- **Inconsistent** - Quality varies across employees
- **Static** - Documents don't guide or track progress
- **Unsupported** - New hires get stuck without immediate help

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**Upload → AI Analyzes → Instant Onboarding Workflow**

1. **Upload** a company document (handbook, policies, procedures)
2. **AI analyzes** the content using Google Gemini
3. **Generates** structured, sequential onboarding steps
4. **Guides** users through each step with detailed instructions
5. **Assists** via AI chatbot for instant help
6. **Tracks** progress and completion automatically
