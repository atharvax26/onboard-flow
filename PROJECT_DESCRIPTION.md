# Onboard Flow - Project Description

## 🎯 What is Onboard Flow?

**Onboard Flow** is an intelligent, AI-powered onboarding platform that transforms static company documents into dynamic, personalized onboarding experiences. Instead of manually creating onboarding steps, companies simply upload their PDF documents, and our AI automatically generates a complete, structured onboarding workflow.

---

## ✨ Complete Feature List

### 1. AI-Powered Document Processing
- **PDF Upload & Parsing** - Upload any company document (policies, handbooks, procedures)
- **Intelligent Text Extraction** - Automatically extracts and analyzes document content
- **Smart Step Generation** - Google Gemini AI creates structured onboarding steps
- **Automatic Sequencing** - AI determines the logical order of steps
- **Dependency Detection** - Identifies which steps depend on others
- **Context-Aware Instructions** - Generates detailed, actionable guidance for each step

## 🚀 What Does It Do?

### The Problem It Solves

**Traditional Onboarding Challenges:**
- ❌ Manual creation of onboarding steps is time-consuming
- ❌ Steps become outdated when documents change
- ❌ Inconsistent onboarding experiences across employees
- ❌ No tracking of progress or completion
- ❌ New hires get stuck without immediate help
- ❌ HR teams spend hours creating and updating materials
- ❌ No analytics on onboarding effectiveness

### Our Solution

**Onboard Flow automates and enhances the entire process:**

1. **Upload Once, Use Forever**
   - Upload your company handbook, policies, or procedures
   - AI analyzes the content and structure
   - Generates a complete onboarding workflow automatically

2. **Intelligent Step Creation**
   - AI reads your document and identifies key topics
   - Creates logical, sequential steps
   - Generates detailed instructions for each step
   - Determines dependencies between steps

3. **Guided Experience**
   - Users follow a clear, step-by-step path
   - Each step has AI-generated guidance
   - Progress is tracked automatically
   - Help is available via chatbot at any time

4. **Real-Time Assistance**
   - Stuck on a step? Ask the AI chatbot
   - Get instant, context-aware answers
   - References the actual document content
   - Available 24/7 without human intervention

5. **Progress Tracking**
   - See completion percentage in real-time
   - Track time spent on each step
   - Maturity scoring shows organizational readiness
   - Admin dashboard provides oversight

6. **Completion & History**
   - Celebrate achievements with animations
   - Automatically archive completed flows
   - Download professional PDF reports
   - Maintain history of all completions
